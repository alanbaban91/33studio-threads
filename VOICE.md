# 33 Studio: Tone of Voice

We write like the agencies whose work ends up on the Cannes stage: Ogilvy, Saatchi & Saatchi,
M&C Saatchi, McCann, Wolff Olins. Not like an agency that posts "5 tips to grow your brand 🚀".

## The one rule
**One idea, said so simply it can't be misunderstood.** If a post needs two ideas, it's two posts.
(M&C Saatchi calls it brutal simplicity. Ogilvy said it as "the headline is 80 cents of your dollar".)

## Five principles
1. **Truth first.** Every post starts from something true that clients feel but rarely say.
   We say it out loud. That's the hook.
2. **Headline-led.** The first line has to work alone, like a poster. If someone reads only that
   line, they should still get it.
3. **Wit, not jokes.** We're clever about the work, never at someone's expense. A smile, not a laugh track.
4. **Confidence is quiet.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "amazing", no hashtags.
   Strong brands don't shout; they don't have to.
5. **Behaviour over decoration.** We talk about ideas, decisions and how brands act.
   We never brag about pretty pictures or our awards. The thinking is the flex.

## Our three moods
| Mood | What it does | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Friendly** | Warm and human. Respects founders, celebrates the craft, invites a conversation. | "To the founder who designed their own sign: respect." |
| **Roast** | Calls out a lazy habit in branding and marketing. Roasts the habit, never a person or a named brand. | "“Clean” is what you call a design you can't remember." |
| **Guide** | Gives away real craft knowledge, generously, before asking for anything. | "Write the problem, not the order." |

Mix per batch: about a third of each. About a third of posts get cards. One or two threads a week.

## The ask
- Quiet, specific and last. One of: `33studio.org`, `hello@33studio.org`, "we're on Salim Street".
- Not every post sells. Roughly half carry an ask; the other half earn the right to.
- Never: "DM us now", "link in bio", "limited spots", "don't miss out".

## Words we don't use
amazing, stunning, elevate, unlock, game-changer, cutting-edge, synergy, passionate,
"in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "we've got you covered", any emoji, any hashtag.

Don't invent clients, numbers, awards or years in business.
**Never name or quote clients.** No client names, logos, testimonials or project details. We talk about what we do, not who we did it for.

## Check before it goes in the queue
- Would the first line work as a poster?
- Could a competitor post this unchanged? If yes, rewrite it.
- Is there a word you could cut? Cut it.
- Did we say one thing, or three?
