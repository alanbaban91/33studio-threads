# 33 Studio: Image Directions, October–November 2026 (15 directions)

Built from the Affinity mood board, filtered through the culture strategy in `VOICE.md`.

## What the mood board says
The board has two families:

1. **The single-object metaphor** (chilli on a red table, calculator, paper-clipped folders, locker,
   cinema seats, retro computer with sticky notes). One tactile object, photographed like a product,
   in a single-colour world, with a bold headline. This is the craft.
2. **Documentary culture** (people pressed against glass, newspaper on the metro, black-and-white
   studio life, team portrait grid, escalator from above). Real people, candid, editorial. This is
   the culture.

**What we keep:** one idea per image, tactile real objects, a single-colour set, editorial black and
white for people, generous empty space for the headline.
**What we drop:** purple glass cards with glow (Vevox), puzzle pieces and "We just FIT" puns, red
as the brand colour, anything that looks like a template.

## The 33 direction: "Noticed by hand"
A real object or a real moment from Sulaymaniyah, shot like a museum piece in the 33 palette.
Then our **hand-drawn gesture marks** are added on top in post: an arm reaching for the tea glass,
a hand pointing at a sign. The mark is the studio's hand touching the city.

- **Palette:** teal `#53E6D4`, deep teal `#033335`, ink `#101616`, cream `#F5F5DD`.
  Single-colour sets, like the board's red-on-red, but in teal, ink or cream.
- **People:** black and white, candid, documentary grain.
- **Type (added after):** Barlow Condensed Black headline, Bold for the line under it, 33 logo bottom left.
- **Marks (added after):** one gesture from `assets/marks/`, in cream on dark images or ink on
  light ones, interacting with the object. Never decoration floating in a corner.
- **Format:** 4:5 vertical (1080 × 1350).
- **Rule for every prompt:** no text and no letters in the generated image. AI can't draw Kurdish
  properly, and type is added by us.

Shared ending for every prompt (paste after each one):
> vertical 4:5, editorial agency poster photograph, high-end product photography, soft directional
> window light, deep soft shadows, matte tactile surfaces, subtle film grain, calm and minimal,
> generous empty space in the {top/bottom} third for a headline, no text, no letters, no logos,
> no watermark

Negative prompt (for tools that support one):
> text, letters, typography, logo, watermark, glossy plastic, glassmorphism, neon glow, purple,
> red, clutter, stock photo look, cartoon, 3D render look, oversaturated, extra fingers

## 1. The Tea Glass (The City)
**Pairs with:** "The tea house was the original co-working space."
**Prompt:** A single traditional Kurdish tea glass (slim tulip-shaped istikan) filled with deep amber
tea on a small white saucer with two sugar cubes, a thin curl of steam, placed on a plinth in a fully
deep-teal (#033335) room, teal walls and floor, one beam of warm window light across the glass,
empty space in the top third.
**Mark:** #10 (reaching wave), cream, an arm reaching in from the left towards the glass.
**Headline:** The original co-working space.

## 2. The Bazaar Palette (The City)
**Pairs with:** "Every bazaar has a colour palette nobody designed."
**Prompt:** Overhead flat lay of five perfect conical heaps of spices (turmeric yellow, sumac
crimson, dried chilli orange, dried mint green, black pepper) arranged in a neat row like paint
swatches on a matte ink-black (#101616) surface, a few grains scattered, hard soft-box light from
above, empty space in the bottom third.
**Mark:** #20 (idea / lightbulb), cream, top right, as if the idea came from the colours.
**Headline:** Every bazaar has a palette nobody designed.

## 3. The Hand-Painted Sign (The Script)

a tea seller's cart in the foreground, film grain, straight-on composition, empty space in the
bottom third.
**Mark:** #6 (pointing arm), cream, pointing up at the sign.
**Headline:** Our city's real type foundry.

## How this becomes a post
1. Generate the base image (or shoot it), 4:5, no text.
2. Drop it into `photos/` with the direction number, e.g. `photos/01-tea-glass.jpg`.
3. The gesture mark, headline and 33 logo get composited on top in the 33 style, then it goes
   into the queue as an image post next to its paired text.

Real photos beat generated ones for 3, 6 and 9. Culture is believed when it's real.
